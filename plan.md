## Content Requirements

**Report structure should tell the full project story** (per Stage 3 guidance), covering:
- **Abstract** – summary of the whole report, placed at the very beginning
- Introduction to the problem/topic
- **Literature Review** (critical, not just descriptive — see marking criteria)
- **Methodology** – research design and/or software development methodology used, with justification
- Project Planning / SRS (Software Requirements Specification)
- **Design Specification** – system architecture, design patterns
- **Implementation** – the actual software product (any language/database, proper coding standards, testing methods)
- **Analysis and Discussion** of results/solution
- **Conclusion** – reflect on whether objectives were achieved, reliability/validity of results
- **Appendix**: signed supervisor meeting logs (minimum 5 meetings required)
